## Math Prerequisites

### Linear Algebra

- [3Blue1Brown - Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) (watch the full series, especially matrix multiplication, transformations, and inverse matrices)
- [Matrix Multiplication Visualized](https://matrixmultiplication.xyz/) (interactive tool to build intuition)
- [Khan Academy - Linear Algebra](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/linear-algebra) (go through matrices and matrix operations sections)

### Multivariable Calculus

- [3Blue1Brown - Essence of Calculus (Multivariable)](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/multivariable-calculus) (gradients, partial derivatives, chain rule in higher dimensions)
- [Khan Academy - Multivariable Calculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/multivariable-calculus) (focus on partial derivatives and gradients sections)
- [Calculus of Several Variables (MIT OCW 18.02)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-02sc-multivariable-calculus-fall-2010/) (lecture 1-10 covers everything you need)

### Quick Reference

If you just need a refresher, these cover the essentials fast:

- [Linear Algebra Review (Stanford CS229)](https://cs229.stanford.edu/notes2022fall/main_notes.pdf) (Appendix A)
- [Calculus Review (Stanford CS229)](https://cs229.stanford.edu/notes2022fall/main_notes.pdf) (Appendix B)
